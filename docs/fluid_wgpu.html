A position-based fluid (PBF) whose neighbour search runs through the kit — every particle asks for the ones inside its kernel radius, every step, and a fluid is about as clustered as data gets. Drag with the mouse or a finger to stir it. M switches the index (Morton grid ↔ kept tree ↔ linear quadtree) and the bars show where the frame goes: maintain vs query vs physics.
